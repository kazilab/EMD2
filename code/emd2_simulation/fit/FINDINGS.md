# Current EMD2 findings and interpretation

The main result is now an exploratory secondary analysis of published functional experiments, not an illustrative-model prediction. Source: [Roje et al., Nature (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07754-w). Exact estimates, unit-level observations, intervals, tests and audit decisions are in `emd2_empirical_summary.json`.

1. **Community intervention.** Three-member versus two-member caecal BCPN has a ratio of arithmetic means of 3.49 (pointwise 95% animal-bootstrap interval 2.37–5.06; eight animals per arm). The mean difference is 2.39 source-table µM (1.49–3.29); exact permutation P = 0.000311, Holm P = 0.00280 across all nine compartments. The rectal difference also survives adjustment. Colon does not (adjusted P = 0.0544). Plasma, liver and kidney contrasts do not establish differences or equivalence. These results concern within-matrix treatment contrasts, not a lumen/plasma source threshold.
2. **Human cultures.** Technical readings are averaged within donor and oxygen condition. All ten donors are equally weighted and paired. Neither oxygen contrast passes the two-test Holm correction; the nine-donor completeness sensitivity does not change that conclusion. Individual culture and isolate activity is descriptive functional evidence; the reanalysis does not establish a population-wide oxygen effect.
3. **Urine and bladder.** Every available week/batch stratum is retained. Effects vary in direction and magnitude. None of the five urinary contrasts passes its family adjustment. One bladder result lies near the Monte Carlo-adjusted 0.05 threshold and is not a decisive finding. No pooled longitudinal effect is estimated.
4. **Source discrepancies.** S39 plasma duplicates are exact copies and collapse safely. S15 caecal duplicates have different values and are excluded; the earlier GF/GFA caecum/plasma summaries are withdrawn. Acute S18 data have inferred treatment labels, count discrepancies and inconsistent published statistics, so they are descriptive only. S37 contains mixed normalisation scales and is excluded from new quantitative inference. No kinetic rate is fitted.
5. **Model role.** The existing 37-state model remains a conditional illustration. Its rates and arm capacities are assigned. Deconjugation drives its antibiotic contrast, and adding oxidation can reduce total systemic exposure, limiting correspondence to the source mechanism. It is neither a calibrated physiological prediction nor independent validation of the experimental evidence.
6. **Source ambiguity.** A baseline host-only caecum/plasma ratio near 0.080 becomes greater than one under assigned exchange or biliary changes. Three host parameters can recover one simulated urinary target. These are model counterexamples, not estimated physiological rates. The legacy 0.0227/h biliary grid point remains only for reproducibility; its earlier claimed match to an ambiguous germ-free ratio is withdrawn.
7. **Conditional recovery.** Synthetic parameter profiles fix most nuisance kinetics and do not establish empirical identifiability. Acquisition counts are not animals. Known liver-filename/colon-label conflicts remain flagged in the original MetaboLights metadata.
8. **KCC scope.** The empirical evidence supports EMD2 through measured microbial transformation and a reconstructed-community intervention. It is relevant to metabolic activation under KCC1, without a new electrophilicity assay. KCC6 requires functional chronic-inflammatory evidence; KCC7 immunosuppression; KCC8 receptor-mediated effects; KCC10 altered proliferation, death or nutrient supply; KCC2 genotoxicity. No such endpoint is imported here. The model exposure index is exactly proportional to urinary excretion and supplies no additional host-response evidence.

**Stated limitations are single-sourced.** The canonical sentences live in the `limits` block of `emd2_empirical_summary.json`, emitted by `empirical.py`. The Methods/Results Limitations section and the supplement quote them verbatim; edit the block, never a document. Two points that the numbered items above imply but do not state outright are included there explicitly: the model's magnitudes are *refuted* rather than merely illustrative, having been compared against published values and found two to three orders high with the intraluminal ordering wrong; and a family that does not survive multiplicity adjustment is not evidence of no effect at these group sizes, where several intervals admit both large and negligible values.

The discriminating strategy remains paired lumen and systemic parent/product measurements, with early temporal resolution and functional microbial perturbations. Neither a terminal concentration ratio nor the currently unresolved acute time course establishes lumen-first versus host-first generation. Taxonomic shifts alone are insufficient.
